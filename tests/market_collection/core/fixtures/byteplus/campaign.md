<span id="frequently-asked-questions"></span>
## Frequently asked questions

**Is every eligible API call during the campaign period billed at the discounted price?** 

For Seedance 2.0 fast and Seedance 2.0 mini, if the usage is billed during the campaign period and the corresponding model has not reached its discount cap, it is charged at the campaign price automatically.

**Do I need to claim or apply for the discount?** 

No. After you meet the campaign requirements, eligible pay\-as\-you\-go usage is automatically billed at the discounted price.

**Can I receive the discount if I have already purchased a resource pack?** 

Resource packs are prepaid quotas purchased at the list price and are deducted before pay\-as\-you\-go charges. The discount does not apply to usage offset by a resource pack. After the resource pack quota is exhausted, or when no resource pack is available, eligible pay\-as\-you\-go usage is billed at the campaign price.

**Does an AI Savings Plan offset the amount before or after the campaign discount?** 

It offsets the discounted amount. The pay\-as\-you\-go charge is first calculated using the applicable price when the usage is billed, and the AI Savings Plan then offsets that discounted charge.

**Is pay\-as\-you\-go billing more cost\-effective than a resource pack during the campaign?** 

Yes. We recommend using pay\-as\-you\-go billing. Pay\-as\-you\-go billing is suitable for on\-demand or fluctuating usage, while resource packs are more suitable for stable usage when you prefer to prepay for quota. Estimate your usage before selecting a billing method to avoid unused resource pack quota expiring.
